HOW IT WORKS

From screenshot to fold animation in four steps

No sign-up and no upload: the studio runs entirely in your browser, so your unreleased screens never leave your computer.

    Add your screens

  2. 02

    Choose a 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 or 9:16 frame, rotate and tilt the phone, and pick a transparent, solid, gradient or photo background.

SCREEN SIZES

Two screens, two shapes

The iPhone Duo has a landscape inner display and a portrait cover display. Export your screens at these sizes for a perfect fit; anything else can be cropped or fitted with one click.

Big screen (inner)

Landscape, about 1.42 : 1

2048 × 1440

Shown when the phone is open. The incoming card slides in on the left half while the right half stays sharp, so keep key content to the right if you use the fold animation.

Small screen (cover)

Portrait, about 0.69 : 1

1032 × 1500

Shown when the phone is closed and while it turns away. A camera sits in the top-right corner, so leave a little room there.

FAQ

iPhone Duo mockup questions

Is this iPhone Duo mockup generator free?

Yes. It is free with no sign-up, no watermark and no export limit. Everything runs in your browser.

What size should my iPhone Duo screenshots be?

The big inner screen is landscape, about 1.42 : 1, so 2048 × 1440 fits exactly. The small cover screen is portrait, about 0.69 : 1, so use 1032 × 1500. Other sizes work too: Fill crops to the screen and Fit whole shows everything.

Can I put a video on the iPhone Duo mockup?

Yes. MP4, WebM and MOV screen recordings play muted and loop on either screen, and the exported MP4 keeps them in sync with the fold.

Can I export a mockup with a transparent background?

Yes. Pick the checkered background and download a PNG with full transparency. Videos cannot be transparent, so they render on white or any colour or photo you choose.

What video formats and sizes can I export?

MP4 (H.264, 60 fps) in 1080p, 2K or 4K, framed 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 or 9:16. Browsers without MP4 encoding fall back to WebM.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

No. The studio renders on your device with WebGL; your files never leave your browser.

Is this an official Apple product?

No. Skillry is independent and not affiliated with Apple. The 3D phone model comes from Apple and remains Apple’s property; iPhone is a trademark of Apple Inc.

Can I use it with my AI agent?

Yes. The iPhone Duo Mockup Skill, included with Pro, lets Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other agents open the studio with your screens or render PNG and MP4 files for you, and includes the full source.
