Quick answer

What is an explainer video?

An explainer video shows how a product or feature works in a minute or two, so a viewer understands it without reading docs. Product explainers stay close to the real interface; animated explainers lean on motion graphics to carry the idea.

Product explainer
The real interface, one feature at a time
Animated explainer
Motion graphics and type that carry the idea
You provide
Product name or URL, and the feature to explain
Typical output
MP4 at 1080p; some Skills offer 2K or 4K
Runs on
Node.js, FFmpeg and a browser renderer on your machine
Running cost
Rendering is local; optional paid stills or music

Explainer video maker FAQ

What these Skills do not do

  • They do not make whiteboard character animation.
  • They do not include voiceover narration by default.
  • They do not record your real app; interface scenes are rebuilt from your inputs.

Is this an online explainer video maker?

No. These are Skills your AI agent runs on your computer. You describe the product and approve the storyboard; the agent builds and renders the video.

How long are the videos?

It depends on the Skill. Cue Style Launch Video makes 60 to 90 second films; most HyperFrames launch films run 20 to 45 seconds.

Do the videos have a voiceover?

Not by default. They use kinetic type and music. Add narration afterwards in your editor if you need it.
