Mascot generator FAQ

What these Skills do not do

  • They do not make physical mascot costumes.
  • They do not deliver editable vector logos.
  • They do not rig or animate the character.

Is the mascot generator free?

Yes. GrokBot Avatar is free and runs on Codex’s built-in image generation, within your account’s usage limits. No external API key is needed.

Can I make an anime-style avatar?

Yes. Ask for a cute soft-vinyl anime avatar; the Skill uses eight large-headed toy references with simple eyes and matte surfaces.

Can I build a mascot by hand instead?

Yes. Dot Avatar Maker is a free browser tool where you pick a shape, colour and expression and export a transparent PNG.

Can I use the mascot as my logo?

You get raster images, not an editable vector logo. Many brands redraw the final character as a vector for logo use.
