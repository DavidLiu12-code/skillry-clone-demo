Compare case studies, navigation and contact pages

A portfolio is judged on its case studies, so compare those first: how a project is told (brief, process, outcome), how visitors move between projects, and how they get in touch.

Portfolio website template FAQ

What these Skills do not do

  • They do not include Figma source files.
  • They are not galleries for photographers with hundreds of images.
  • Contact forms do not send email until you connect a backend.

What should a portfolio website include?

At minimum a home page, three to six case studies with outcomes, an about page and a clear way to contact you. These Skills include all of them.

Is there an AI portfolio builder here?

Your AI agent is the builder: the Skill gives it the design and structure, you give it your projects, and it writes the finished site.

Can I see portfolio website examples first?

Yes. Every card opens a live demo of the full site, including case-study pages and the phone layout.

Do they work for artists?

Collage Creative Portfolio suits visual and art work. For large photo galleries, a gallery-first site will serve you better.
