Quick answers

Claude Motion in 30 seconds

What is Claude Motion?

A Claude feature that animates text, charts, shapes and images with code, so you can edit the result and export an MP4.

Who can use it?

Claude Team and Enterprise plans, while it is in beta. Free, Pro and Max plans do not include it.

Is it an AI video generator?

No. It animates content you provide. It does not generate filmed footage or realistic people.

Does it cost extra?

There is no separate price. Each request counts toward your Claude plan's usage limits.

The basics

What is Claude Motion?

Claude Motion is part of Anthropic's October 2026 Dashboards and Motion release. Add your text, numbers or images and Claude writes the animation as code, so you can keep asking for changes.

Export the result as an MP4 for a deck, a post or your video editor. The same request can also become an interactive Claude dashboard.

Official announcement: Build live dashboards and animate explainers with Claude

Good for

  • Animated charts and data stories
  • Explainers built from your own text
  • Product walkthroughs with shapes and screenshots
  • Title cards, lists and comparisons

Not designed for

  • A text-to-video model for filmed scenes
  • A tool that invents realistic people or voices
  • A full video editor with tracks and audio mixing
  • Available on Claude Free, Pro or Max today

Step by step

How to use Claude Motion

To use Claude Motion, follow the four steps from the official help article below. We describe the documented flow; the step panels are diagrams, not product captures.

  1. Output → Motion01
    Step 1

    Start a Motion

    Ask for an animation in a normal chat, choose Output → Motion, or start from a Motion template in Artifacts. The announcement also shows a /motion shortcut.

  2. Mon 4.8k · Tue 4.6k02
    Step 2

    Add your content

    Paste the script, numbers or bullet points, and attach images or a logo. Say who will watch it and how long it should be.

  3. "hold the last frame"03
    Step 3

    Edit by asking

    Ask for changes in plain words: "make the bars grow one by one", "hold the last frame for two seconds", "use our brand blue".

  4. animation.mp404
    Step 4

    Export the MP4

    Export the finished animation as MP4 and place it in your deck, post or video timeline.

Tips for better results

  • One idea per animation. Split a long story into several short motions.
  • Give exact numbers and labels. The animation uses exactly what you provide.
  • State the format: length in seconds, 16:9 or 9:16, light or dark.
  • Ask for a held final frame so viewers can read the result.

In depth

When Claude Motion is the right tool

01

Short explainers from facts you already have

It works best when the content exists: a set of numbers, a list of steps, a before-and-after. It is quick to ask for an animated version and to iterate on timing in the same chat, without opening a timeline editor.

02

Brand-consistent motion across a team

On Team and Enterprise plans everyone works in the same Claude workspace, so a shared prompt with your colours and type can keep motion graphics consistent from one person to the next.

03

Claude Motion vs AI video generators

The official feature animates content you give it, so a chart shows your real numbers and every label can be changed later. AI video generators such as Runway or Veo create new footage from a prompt: great for scenes and people, but they will not draw your chart accurately.

04

When another route fits better

If you need filmed scenes, realistic people or voices, an AI video generator fits better. If you need hundreds of videos from a spreadsheet, a video API is built for that. If your plan has Claude Code but not Claude Motion, a motion Skill gives you a similar animation as an editable project.

FAQ

Claude Motion FAQ

Is Claude Motion free?

No. It is not on the Free plan. On Team and Enterprise plans there is no separate price, but every request counts toward the plan's usage limits.

Can I use Claude Motion on Claude Pro or Max?

Not as of October 16, 2026; it is a Team and Enterprise beta. Pro and Max do include Claude Code, so you can make code-based motion graphics there with a motion Skill. Our Claude Motion alternatives guide shows the options.

Will Claude Motion come to Pro, Max or Free?

Anthropic has not announced a date. As of October 16, 2026 the help article lists it as a Team and Enterprise beta. Until that changes, Pro and Max users can use Claude Code with a motion Skill instead.

Is Claude Motion an AI video generator?

No. It animates text, charts, shapes and images that you provide, using code. It does not generate filmed footage, realistic people or voices.

Can I export Claude Motion animations as MP4?

Yes. The help article says you can edit an animation and export it as an MP4 file for slides, social posts or a video editor.

How do I get and open Claude Motion?

Ask for an animation in a chat, choose Output → Motion, or start from a Motion template in Artifacts. If you are on Enterprise and do not see it, ask your Owner to enable it in the Artifacts settings.

What is the difference between Claude Motion and Remotion?

Remotion is a React framework for making videos with code, which developers set up and render themselves. Claude Motion is a feature inside Claude: you describe the animation and Claude writes it for you.

Does Claude Motion work in Claude Code?

No, it lives in the Claude apps. In Claude Code you can get a similar result with a motion Skill that writes the animation project and renders an MP4 on your machine.

Can I make Claude Motion-style videos for YouTube?

Yes, in Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. CreatorMotion has 122+ motion effects made for talking-head videos, such as text behind you and split-screen charts, and 2 of them are free. For launch and product videos, LaunchMotion has 208 scenes.

Is Skillry part of Anthropic?

No. Skillry is an independent marketplace for AI agent Skills. Claude and its Motion feature are products of Anthropic. This page explains the official feature and links to Anthropic's own documentation.
